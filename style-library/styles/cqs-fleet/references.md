## Lighting aspiration

A user-supplied package of earlier concept art (the "Via Siderum" designs) set the lighting target.
Its useful part is the light language, not its shapes:
- dark hulls with a few bright, short, hard-edged amber slits at block corners and in recesses;
- dim warm windows;
- almost no scattered dots.

Its procedural Blender renders were the quality baseline to beat. The package is not in the repo. Use
it only as a lighting and mood reference; never copy its shapes.
