# Brief: Spaceport (orbital building)

**Game source:**
- `BuildingEnum.SPACEPORT`: "Spaceports are orbital shipyards that were created to build large
  spaceships. They are extremely important for interstellar travel, opening the whole universe to you."
- Cost tier: points 35, upkeep 4000, 3100 construction jobs, the longest build time.
- It builds the warp-capable units: the 900 m CV-50 carrier, warp freighters and warp variants.

**Scale:**
- **Biggest product:** a 900 x 406 x 319 m carrier, so the main dock frame clears about 1,000 x 450 x
  350 m.
- **Also on the station:**
  - berths for 134 m freighters delivering material;
  - fabrication modules;
  - crew habitats (thousands of workers);
  - power (solar and reactor) and radiators;
  - docking for the fleet.
- **Overall:** about 1.5-2 km.
- **Rulers:** the style's rulers (1 m ports on 3 m decks, doors, lamps of one fixed size) must be
  present, so the station reads as vast next to the carrier.

**Must read as:** an orbital industrial station from the same shipyards as the fleet. Signals:
- naval-industrial construction;
- the fleet's markings;
- the amber slit and pin lights, plus windows only where crew live.

Its construction scaffolding and gantries must look different from the finished hulls.

**Open design choices** (to show in the spread):
- an open lattice dry-dock frame round the hull, vs an enclosed pressurised dock hall, vs a spine
  station with several berths, vs a ring with radial docks;
- light thermal paint (stations cannot hide) vs the fleet's dark grey.

**Where it is seen:** the orbital fleet scene (400 km above the planet), with a carrier under
construction in the dock, plus the studio view.
