# Brief: Spaceport (orbital building)

**Game source:**
- `BuildingEnum.SPACEPORT`: "Spaceports are orbital shipyards that were created to build large
  spaceships. They are extremely important for interstellar travel, opening the whole universe to you."
- Cost tier: points 35, upkeep 4000, 3100 construction jobs, the longest build time.
- It builds the warp-capable units: the 900 m CV-50 carrier, warp freighters and warp variants.

**Round 1 rejected (user, 2026-10-01, correction 21):** "I don't particularly like either design, tbh.
Can you try a few more?" Do not show these forms again:
- an open box-girder dry dock round the hull;
- an enclosed dock hall;
- a long spine with berths;
- a polygonal ring with radial docks.

No single dislike was named. All four were a dock frame round a finished-looking carrier, and the carrier
took the identity.

**The station is the hero (round 2):**
- The station must be recognisable without the ship.
- If a carrier is shown, it is clearly under construction (keel, frames, part of the plating) and does
  not own the silhouette.

**Compact, thumbnail-first (correction 22).** The game shows the building as a square icon (80 and
40 px):
- one dominant massing about 1.5:1 at most from the icon camera, not a long spine;
- one or two signature shapes that read at 40 px;
- a light / dark value split that survives downscaling;
- rich detail as the second layer, for close shots.

**Scale:**
- **Biggest product:** a 900 x 406 x 319 m carrier, so the build bay clears about 1,000 x 450 x 350 m.
- **Also on the station:**
  - fabrication;
  - crew habitats (thousands of workers);
  - power and radiators;
  - berths for 134 m freighters delivering material;
  - docking for the fleet.
- **Overall:** lean, about 1.0-1.3 km, only modestly larger than the carrier it builds (correction
  25: "It shouldn't be several times larger than the carrier for no apparent reason. Make it much
  leaner"). Every big element has a job; no bulk for its own sake.
- **Round 2 (2026-10-01):** too massive. Show the real CV-50 part-built (correction 26) in light paint
  (correction 27), with lights and small details on.
- **Rulers:** the style's rulers (1 m ports on 3 m decks, doors, lamps of one fixed size) must be
  present, so the station reads as vast.

**Must read as:** an orbital industrial station from the same shipyards as the fleet. Signals:
- naval-industrial construction;
- the fleet's markings;
- amber slit and pin lights, with windows only where crew live.

Its construction scaffolding and gantries must look different from the finished hulls.

**Where it is seen:**
- the game's square building icon (80 and 40 px);
- the orbital fleet scene (400 km above the planet);
- the studio view.
