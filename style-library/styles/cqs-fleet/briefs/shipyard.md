# Brief: Shipyard (planetary building)

**Game source:**
- `BuildingEnum.SHIPYARD`: "Units, which are constructed here, are not planet-bound anymore. They
  have the possibility of moving in the interplanetary neighbourhood, expanding trading routes and
  giving you advantages when fighting enemies."
- Cost tier: points 30, upkeep 3000, 1000 construction jobs.
- `Colony.getCorrectUnitQueue`: the shipyard builds every non-warp space unit:
  - fighter (71.7 m);
  - corvette (108 m);
  - civil ships (134 m);
  - destroyer (203 m).
- The SPACEPORT is explicitly orbital, so the shipyard is the **surface** (planetside) yard.

**Scale:**
- **Biggest product:** a 203 m x 56 m x 74 m destroyer, so an assembly bay or berth needs about 240 x
  80 m of floor and about 90 m of clear height.
- **Smaller berths:** fighters and corvettes.
- **Site:** with fabrication halls, a fuel (deuterium) farm, a control tower, crew housing and
  logistics, the site is about 1-1.5 km across.
- **Rulers:** the style's human rulers (doors, rails, 20-ft containers, deck tractors) must appear,
  so its size reads.

**Must read as:** a vast heavy-industrial planetside yard of the same civilisation. Signals:
- faceted, chamfered, layered construction;
- the fleet's markings language;
- the amber light language at night.

**The key design question** (to show in the spread): how a 200 m ship leaves the ground. The fleet's
ships have aft fusion bells and no landing gear. Options:
- lift-off pads with blast pits and launch cradles;
- a mass-driver launch rail;
- a vertical assembly-and-launch tower;
- covered assembly halls that open onto a launch apron.

**Where it is seen:** studio / overview camera (an elevated three-quarter view of the site with a ship
in a berth for scale). A ground diorama scene does not exist yet.
