# Brief: Shipyard (planetary building)

**Game source:**
- `BuildingEnum.SHIPYARD`: "Units, which are constructed here, are not planet-bound anymore. They
  have the possibility of moving in the interplanetary neighbourhood, expanding trading routes and
  giving you advantages when fighting enemies."
- Cost tier: points 30, upkeep 3000, 1000 construction jobs.
- `Colony.getCorrectUnitQueue`: the shipyard builds every non-warp space unit:
  - fighter (71.7 m);
  - corvette (108 m);
  - civil ships (134 m);
  - destroyer (203 m).
- The SPACEPORT is explicitly orbital, so the shipyard is the **surface** (planetside) yard.

**Not a launch facility (user, 2026-10-01, correction 20).** In the game, ships land and take off under
their own power on any planet. The yard does not show or explain how:
- no launch rails, cradles, launch towers, blast pits or crawlers;
- no design question about reaching orbit.

A finished ship can simply stand in the yard. The r1 spread was organised round launch hardware and is
superseded.

**What it is:** the place where spaceships are built and fitted out: assembly halls or berths, cranes and
gantries, hull sections and modules being fabricated and moved in, a ship under construction.

**Compact, thumbnail-first (correction 22).** The game shows the building as a square icon (80 and
40 px):
- The yard reads as **one dominant structure** with a clear outline, with the rest packed tight round it:
  about 300-450 m across, and about 1.5:1 at most from the icon camera (three-quarter, about 30° up).
- The r1 sites (1.3 km of similar sheds) failed this: at 40 px they are a grey smear.
- At icon size, one or two signature shapes say "spaceships are built here". Examples: a ship's
  hull visible inside a giant gantry or arch, a monumental hall with a bow nosing out, a crane silhouette.
- Rich detail stays for close shots: rulers, markings, cranes, containers and vehicles.

**Round 2 (2026-10-01), correction 24:** "something between A and E" (A's portal gantry, E's
hammerhead cranes); the DD-12 visibly less complete; lighting and dense small details on; darker overall,
with depth. Keep the real DD-12 (correction 26) and light paint (correction 27).

**Scale:**
- **Biggest product:** a 203 m x 56 m x 74 m destroyer, so the main bay or berth needs about 240 x 80 m
  of floor and about 90 m of clear height.
- **Rulers:** the style's human rulers (doors, rails, 20-ft containers, trucks, the 1.8 m figure) must
  appear, so its size reads.

**Must read as:** a heavy-industrial planetside shipyard of the same civilisation. Signals:
- faceted, chamfered, layered construction;
- the fleet's markings language;
- the amber light language at night.

**Where it is seen:**
- the game's square building icon (80 and 40 px);
- a studio / overview camera: an elevated three-quarter view with a ship under construction for scale.

A ground diorama scene does not exist yet.

**Picked (correction 28): r3 A**, the gate-led yard at dusk (`images/spread-shipyard-r3-A.jpg`), with the
sea ship replaced by the part-built DD-12.
